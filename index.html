Onus Support

Earn the right to scroll.

Onus seals the apps that eat your time. Open one and it asks for a price first: push-ups, a walk, or your phone face down. Then it's yours for a while. You choose the price.

Email support

I forgot my exit code

It can't be recovered: Onus keeps only a fingerprint of it, never the digits. You can always switch Onus off instead. iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access → Onus. Android: Settings → Accessibility → Onus. Or delete Onus. Those doors stay open on purpose.

Push-ups aren't counting

Stand your phone up on the floor about a metre away, screen facing you, so it sees your shoulders and arms: you see yourself on screen, with your joints drawn in red. Use a lit room. Onus tells you when it's too dark, when you're too close, or when it can't see your hands. Go all the way down and all the way up.

Can I still call and text?

Always. Phone, Messages and emergency calls are never sealed.

Can I change the price?

Yes: how many push-ups (10 to 50), how many steps (250 to 2,000), how long face down (3 to 10 minutes) and how long an app opens for (10 to 60 minutes), in Settings → Challenges. Harder starts at once. Easier starts tomorrow, so it's never a way out in a weak moment.

Why does each open cost more?

Each time you earn the same app in a day, its challenge rises a little, up to double. It resets at midnight.

What does Onus see?

Nothing leaves your phone. Onus has no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
