Onus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Onus collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything Onus knows stays on your phone.

Onus is an app for iPhone and Android that seals the apps you choose: open one and Onus asks for a challenge first (push-ups, a short walk or a few minutes with your phone face down), then the app opens for 30 minutes. This policy explains what the app uses on your phone and why. It's made by Andrej Gazi in Leicester, UK.

The camera

For push-ups, Onus looks through the front camera and works out where your shoulders, elbows and wrists are, on your phone (on iPhone with Apple's own Vision framework, on Android with Google ML Kit running on the device). While you do them, Onus shows you the camera's live picture with those joints drawn over it, so you can see you're in view. That picture is only shown on your screen: no picture or video is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The camera is on only while a push-up challenge is open.

Motion and steps

For the walk, Onus reads your phone's own step counter. For face down, it reads which way the phone is lying. These readings are used on the phone, while a challenge is open, and are not kept.

Which apps are sealed

On iPhone, Onus uses Apple's Screen Time. You pick apps in Apple's own picker, and Apple gives Onus only anonymous tokens for them, never their names. Apple's shield screen shows an app's name; Onus keeps that name on your phone to say, for example, "Earn 30 minutes of Instagram?".

On Android, Onus uses an accessibility service only to notice which app has just opened, so it can step in first. It doesn't read what is on your screen, what you type, or your messages. The Android app has no permission to use the internet at all.

What Onus keeps on your phone

  • The apps you sealed, and how long each has left.
  • Today's and this week's challenges (push-ups, steps, minutes face down, minutes earned).
  • Your settings, and how far you got in the first run.
  • Your exit code, stored only as a salted fingerprint (SHA-256), never the digits.

Deleting Onus deletes all of it.

Onus Pro

Onus Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google take the payment: Onus never sees your card or your account details. To know whether Onus Pro is active, Onus asks the store app on your phone; nothing about it goes to us.

Notifications and sharing

On iPhone, when you tap Earn it on a sealed app, Onus shows a notification made on your phone. If you choose Share your week, Onus hands a picture of your week to your phone's own share sheet, and you decide where it goes.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • No analytics, and no third-party libraries that send data.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because nothing is collected.

Children

Onus doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.
